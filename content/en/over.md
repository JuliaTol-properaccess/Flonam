Flonam develops SaaS products for businesses and organisations. Each
product is a standalone solution for one specific problem, with its
own name and its own website.

## Why separate products

Every Flonam product operates independently of the others. There's
no bundled package and no long-term commitment: data stays the
customer's property and can be taken along at any time.

## Team

Flonam is a young company. Its founders each bring their own
background: one in digital accessibility, the other in commercial
operations.
